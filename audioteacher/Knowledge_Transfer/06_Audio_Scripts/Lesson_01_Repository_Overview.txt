Welcome back student. Today we are going to explore the Enterprise Sales Analytics Data Warehouse repository.
Corporate sales organizations face complex challenges tracking multi-line orders, manager revenue attribution, and fulfillment location rules.
This repository implements a Kimball Star Schema data warehouse with SCD Type 2 dimensions, many-to-many bridge tables, and automated metric reconciliation.
Let's pause and understand this with an example. Imagine a large enterprise account managed by two regional sales managers. If both claim 100 percent of a 100 thousand dollar order, revenue gets double counted. Our bridge table assigns fractional allocation weights to ensure perfect financial accuracy.
Now let's see where this appears in the repository. Pipeline execution starts in main dot py, which coordinates schema migrations and ETL loaders inside src etl.
